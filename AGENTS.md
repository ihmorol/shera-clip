## Product authority

Read `docs/PRD.md`, `docs/decisions.md`, and the relevant gates in `docs/acceptance.md` before implementation. `PRODUCT.md` and `docs/ui-theme.md` guide interface work. The analyses in `plans/` and `.omx/plans/` are historical and cannot override the PRD.

The owner must decide changes to product scope, clip-quality rules, UI wording/layout, external spend, or acceptance criteria. Choose routine technical details within those bounds. Do not invent class media, human labels, brand assets, API credentials, or verification results. Keep media, transcripts, exports, and secrets out of GitHub.
